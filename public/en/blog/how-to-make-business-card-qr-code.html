How to Make a Business Card QR Code

November 8, 2026 · QR Genius Editorial Team

A QR code on a business card can help someone save your contact details without typing them manually. One practical format is a vCard QR code, which encodes fields such as your name, phone number, email address and organization.

1. Choose the contact information to share

Use your professional name and the contact details you want recipients to save. Double-check your email address, international phone format, company name and website URL. Only include information you are comfortable distributing publicly on a printed card.

2. Generate a vCard QR code

Open the free business card QR code generator. Fill in the relevant fields and generate the code. Unlike a website QR code, a vCard QR code can let compatible devices display a contact card or offer to save contact information directly.

3. Scan and inspect the saved contact

Use a second phone or ask a colleague to scan the code. Check every field in the preview: name, telephone, email, company and website. Different camera and contact apps may handle optional fields differently. If a phone cannot import the vCard, offer your contact information in readable text as a fallback.

4. Prepare the card for printing

Use a crisp, high-contrast QR code and keep clear white space around it. Avoid placing the code on a fold, textured background or glossy area that reflects light. A QR code around 2.5–3 cm wide can be a useful starting point on a typical business card, but scan reliability depends on data density, print quality and camera distance. Always test an actual printed proof.

5. Add a clear call to action

Put a short label next to the code, such as “Scan to save my contact.” Keep your name and at least one contact method printed in plain text so the card remains useful without a smartphone.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not encode an outdated number or misspelled email. Avoid cramming unnecessary information into the code, which can make it denser and harder to scan at small sizes. Re-test after changing the design or scaling the artwork.

Can you update a printed vCard QR code?

A static vCard QR code contains the details encoded when it was created. If those details change, generate a new code and replace printed cards. An alternative is a QR code pointing to an online contact page, which may be editable separately, but that depends on keeping the page available.
