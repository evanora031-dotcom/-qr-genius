How to Create a WhatsApp QR Code for Customers

November 8, 2026 · QR Genius Editorial Team

A WhatsApp QR code gives people a convenient way to open a chat with your business. You can place it on a menu, receipt, flyer, product package or business card. The code typically points to a WhatsApp click-to-chat link, so customers can decide whether to send a message.

1. Choose the number customers should contact

Use a phone number that is active on WhatsApp and that your team can monitor. Enter it in full international format, including the country code, but without a leading plus sign, spaces, brackets or dashes. For example, a number formatted as +1 (415) 555-0123 would be entered as 14155550123. This is an illustrative number, not a support contact.

2. Decide whether to add a suggested message

A pre-filled message can help visitors explain why they are reaching out, such as “Hello, I have a question about your menu.” The message appears in the chat composer; the visitor still chooses whether to send it. Avoid asking people to include sensitive personal information in the suggested text.

3. Generate your QR code

Open the free WhatsApp QR code generator, enter the phone number and optional message, and generate the code. The destination is based on a WhatsApp link such as wa.me followed by the international phone digits. Download the QR code for your intended use.

4. Test the destination on a real phone

Scan with a device that has WhatsApp available. Confirm that the correct chat opens and that the optional message is readable. Also test from a printed proof rather than relying only on a screen preview. Some devices may show a browser prompt before opening the app.

5. Make the printed code easy to scan

Use a high-contrast code with a clear quiet zone around it. Keep it away from folds, reflective finishes and very small print. Add a short label such as “Scan to chat with us on WhatsApp” so customers know what will happen.

Common questions

Does scanning automatically send a message?

No. A standard WhatsApp click-to-chat link opens a conversation or draft; the customer must choose to send it.

What happens if the business number changes?

A static QR code points to the old number. Generate and replace printed copies when the number changes.

Can the QR code track who scanned it?

The basic static QR code does not provide scan analytics by itself. Do not promise tracking unless a separate, clearly disclosed service actually provides it.
